About KU Chat House

The official community space for Kampala University students, lecturers, and administrators.

Discuss & Ask

Post questions, share updates, and get answers from peers, lecturers, and admins in one shared feed.

Official Announcements

Administrators can pin important campus announcements straight to the top of the feed.

React to Comments

Comments are shown right on each post, and you can react to the ones that are helpful.

Members Directory

Browse everyone registered on the platform and send a connection request to start chatting.

Private, Consent-Based Messaging

You can only message a member after they've accepted your connection request -- no unsolicited DMs. Attach photos or audio clips right in the chat.

Group Chats

Start a group with people you're connected with -- perfect for study groups and class projects.

Your University ID

Your ID is your permanent login credential and can never be changed. Your username, photo, and bio can be updated anytime.
